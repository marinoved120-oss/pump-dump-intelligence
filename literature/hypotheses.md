# Testable hypotheses

- **H1:** Negative CVD slope near the local high adds predictive information beyond price and volume.
- **H2:** Rising open interest without continued price appreciation increases forward dump probability.
- **H3:** Falling buy efficiency precedes a distribution phase.
- **H4:** Funding is useful only after normalization to the instrument's own history.
- **H5:** Short windows (1–15 minutes) outperform long windows for dump-onset timing.
- **H6:** A model trained on futures-led altcoins will not transfer unchanged to DEX meme tokens or TradFi perpetuals.
- **H7:** A sequence of independent signals outperforms any single threshold.
- **H8:** Data-quality gating materially reduces false alarms caused by wrong pairs, stale markets and inactive pools.
